# Brand review checklist

## Identity

- Correct logo variant and source asset.
- Correct proportions, clear space, minimum size, and background.
- No unapproved redraw, crop, effect, deformation, or color change.

## Visual system

- Palette and accent frequency match the profile.
- Text contrast and readability meet requirements.
- Display/body type, weights, casing, tracking, and fallback behavior are correct.
- Product UI remains truthful.

## Voice

- Headlines, labels, terminology, and call to action follow the profile.
- Copy is concise and appropriate for the audience.
- No prohibited claims or phrases appear.

## Motion

- Signature behavior is recognizable but not overused.
- Easing, durations, camera, depth, transitions, and beat density match the profile.
- Scene pacing expresses the intended personality.
- Reduced-motion constraints have a viable alternative when applicable.

## Outro

- Approved construction and audio sting.
- Final mark is readable and held for the required duration.
- Any call to action respects safe areas and approved language.

## Exceptions

- Every deliberate deviation is documented in the production brief.
