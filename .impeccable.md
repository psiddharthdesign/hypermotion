## Design Context

### Users

Hyper Motion is for motion and product designers working in a dense desktop
editor. They move quickly between canvas, layers, inspector, and timeline, and
expect keyboard/mouse precision without losing their current selection.

### Use Cases

- Import structured designs and animate semantic properties without breaking
  layout.
- Select specific layers, tracks, or keyframes and edit only that scope.
- Coordinate animation to musical timing, then refine the motion feel of
  individual keyframe sets.
- Preview, iterate, and export without destructive or surprising edits.

### Brand Personality

Precise, calm, and technically confident. The interaction model should feel at
home beside Logic Pro, Jitter, and Figma: compact and professional, never
toy-like, decorative, or crowded.

### Aesthetic Direction

Retain the existing compact editor chrome, theme tokens, typography, and blue
selection accent in both light and dark modes. Prefer progressive disclosure
and small contextual controls over new permanent panels or modal-heavy flows.

### Design Principles

1. Make edit scope explicit before an action changes animation data.
2. Preserve layer, track, and keyframe selection across inspector interactions.
3. Keep common operations one click away; reveal curve-level control only when
   requested.
4. Every animation edit must be undoable and must preserve unrelated timing,
   values, grouping, and stagger metadata.
5. Dense is acceptable; ambiguous is not.
